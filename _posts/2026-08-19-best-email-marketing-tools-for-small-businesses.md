# Best Email Marketing Tools for Small Businesses in 2026

Email is the highest-ROI channel a small business has — but only if you pick a tool you'll actually use. Here are the best for lean teams.

## 1. Mailchimp — best all-in-one for beginners

Mailchimp is the default: email, landing pages, and basic automation in a familiar interface, with a generous free tier.

- **Best for:** businesses starting out that want one tool for email + landing pages.
- **Price:** free tier; from ~$13/month.
- **Trade-off:** gets expensive as your list grows; automation is basic on lower tiers.

## 2. ConvertKit — best for creators and newsletters

ConvertKit is built for creators: simple broadcast emails, visual automation, and easy tagging. The favorite of bloggers and newsletter writers.

- **Best for:** creators, bloggers, and newsletter-first businesses.
- **Price:** free tier (up to 1k); from ~$15/month.
- **Trade-off:** lighter on e-commerce features.

## 3. Brevo (ex-Sendinblue) — best value for volume

Brevo charges by email volume, not subscriber count — a big saving for businesses that email a lot but have a smaller list. Includes SMS and CRM basics.

- **Best for:** high-volume senders on a budget.
- **Price:** free tier (300 emails/day); from ~$9/month.
- **Trade-off:** interface less polished; deliverability requires domain setup.

## 4. Klaviyo — best for e-commerce

Klaviyo is the e-commerce powerhouse: deep segmentation, abandoned-cart flows, and revenue reporting tied to orders.

- **Best for:** online stores that want revenue-driven email.
- **Price:** free tier; from ~$20/month.
- **Trade-off:** overkill for non-e-commerce businesses.

## Comparison at a glance

| Tool | Best for | Price | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mailchimp | Beginners | Free | All-in-one | Pricey at scale |
| ConvertKit | Creators | Free | Simple automation | Lighter e-commerce |
| Brevo | Volume | Free | Pay by volume | Less polished |
| Klaviyo | E-commerce | Free | Revenue-driven | Niche |

## The verdict

- **Just starting, want everything?** Mailchimp.
- **Creator / newsletter?** ConvertKit.
- **Email a lot, small list?** Brevo.
- **Online store?** Klaviyo.
