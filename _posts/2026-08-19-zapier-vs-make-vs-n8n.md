# Zapier vs Make vs n8n: Which Automation Tool Is Best in 2026?

Automation is the biggest time-saver for small teams — but choosing the wrong tool means redoing every workflow later. Here's an honest comparison of the three leaders: **Zapier**, **Make**, and **n8n**.

## The short version

- **Zapier** — easiest to learn, most integrations, priciest at scale.
- **Make** — best balance of power and ease, great visual builder.
- **n8n** — most powerful and cheapest (self-hostable), steeper learning curve.

## Zapier — best for beginners

Zapier is the industry default: 7,000+ integrations and a dead-simple interface. Anyone can build a "Zap" in minutes.

- **Best for:** non-technical teams that want it to "just work".
- **Price:** free tier (100 tasks/mo); from ~$20/month paid.
- **Trade-off:** task-based pricing gets expensive fast; limited logic vs. the others.

## Make — best balance

Make (formerly Integromat) uses a visual scenario builder with real branching logic. More powerful than Zapier, easier than n8n.

- **Best for:** teams that outgrow Zapier but don't want code.
- **Price:** free tier (1,000 ops/mo); from ~$9/month.
- **Trade-off:** fewer integrations than Zapier; UI has a learning curve.

## n8n — best power and price

n8n is open-source and self-hostable — you can run it on your own server for free (unlimited runs, pay only hosting). The most flexible of the three.

- **Best for:** technical teams that want full control and no per-task fees.
- **Price:** free (self-hosted); cloud from ~$24/month.
- **Trade-off:** steeper learning curve; you manage your own infra if self-hosted.
