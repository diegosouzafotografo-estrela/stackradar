# Best AI Email Assistants for Consultants in 2026

Consultants live in their inbox. The average consultant spends **2–3 hours a day** on email — reading, triaging, drafting, and chasing replies. AI email assistants cut that time dramatically, but not every tool fits a consultant's workflow (client-facing tone, follow-up discipline, billable time).

Here are AI email assistants for consultants in 2026, researched from public product information and compared.

## 1. Superhuman — best for speed and triage

Superhuman pairs a blazing-fast interface with AI features (AI triage, instant reply drafting, follow-up reminders). It's the best choice for consultants who send **50+ emails a day** and want to reach inbox zero fast.

- **Best for:** high-volume solo consultants.
- **Price:** from $30/month.
- **Trade-off:** pricey; Gmail/Outlook only.

## 2. Missive — best for teams and shared inboxes

Missive combines a shared inbox, internal chat, and AI drafting. If your consultancy handles client emails as a **team** (shared `hello@` inbox), Missive keeps everyone on the same thread without forwarding chains.

- **Best for:** small consulting teams.
- **Price:** from $14/user/month.
- **Trade-off:** overkill for a solo consultant.

## 3. SaneBox — best for "set and forget" filtering

SaneBox learns your habits and automatically filters noise into folders, surfacing what matters. It works inside your **existing** email client — no new app to learn.

- **Best for:** consultants who don't want to switch inboxes.
- **Price:** from ~$7/month (billed annually).
- **Trade-off:** filtering, not writing — pair it with a drafting tool.

## 4. Flowrite — best for AI drafting in your own voice

Flowrite turns short prompts into full emails in **your tone**. Great for repetitive client outreach, proposals, and follow-ups that need to sound human.

- **Best for:** drafting speed and consistency.
- **Price:** from $15/month.
- **Trade-off:** writing-focused; doesn't manage your inbox.

## 5. Gemini in Gmail — best free native option

If you're on Gmail, Google's built-in Gemini drafts replies and summarizes threads for **$0 extra** (with a Workspace plan). The lowest-effort way to start.

- **Best for:** zero-budget start.
- **Price:** included with Workspace (from ~$7/month).
- **Trade-off:** less polished than dedicated tools; generic tone.

## The verdict

- **Solo consultant, high volume:** Superhuman.
- **Small team:** Missive.

- **Just starting, zero budget:** Gemini in Gmail.
